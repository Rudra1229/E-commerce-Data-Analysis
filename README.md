# E-Commerce Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to understand business performance and identify actionable insights related to sales, profit, customers, regions, categories, sub-categories, discounts, and products.

The project follows a complete data analysis workflow, starting with data cleaning in Excel, followed by exploratory data analysis using Python Pandas, and finally presenting the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the highest-performing categories and sub-categories.
- Compare sales and profitability across different regions.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Identify profitable and loss-making products/sub-categories.
- Understand customer-segment performance.
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Identify the best-performing region and category combinations.
- Convert the analysis into meaningful business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and preparation
- **Python** – Exploratory data analysis
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation, grouping, aggregation, and analysis
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Python analysis environment
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **SQL** – Considered as an analytical approach; Pandas was prioritized for this project

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning – Excel

The raw e-commerce dataset was cleaned and prepared in Excel before analysis.

The cleaning process included checking the dataset, correcting inconsistencies, handling missing or invalid values, and preparing the data for further analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis – Python & Pandas

Pandas was used as the primary tool for exploratory data analysis.

The analysis was structured around important business questions such as:

1. Which category has the highest sales?
2. Which region generates the highest total sales?
3. How do sales change over time?
4. Which category/sub-category generates the highest profit?
5. Which sub-categories or products are generating losses?
6. Which customer segment generates the highest sales and profit?
7. What is the relationship between discount and profit?
8. Which region/category combination performs best in terms of sales and profit?

Pandas was used for:

- Grouping and aggregating data
- Category and sub-category analysis
- Regional analysis
- Customer-segment analysis
- Time-based sales analysis
- Profit and loss analysis
- Discount and profitability analysis
- Comparing regional and category performance

### 3. Power BI Dashboard

The results of the analysis were used to create an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard focuses on important KPIs and visualizations covering:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Sales Trends
- Regional Performance
- Category Performance
- Sub-Category Performance
- Customer Segments
- Product Performance

## Key Business Insights

### Technology is the Leading Category

The **Technology category** emerged as the highest-selling and most profitable category, making it a major contributor to the company's overall performance.

This indicates that Technology is an important area for the company to maintain and potentially expand.

### West Region Leads in Sales

The **West region generates the highest total sales** among the regions analyzed.

This suggests strong customer demand and market performance in the region. The company could consider allocating additional resources to this region to sustain its performance and support future growth.

### Sales Show Strong Growth Over Time

Sales were comparatively lower during the initial period but increased significantly in later years.

This indicates a positive overall growth trend and improving business performance over time.

Understanding the factors behind this growth could help the company maintain the momentum.

### Copiers Generate the Highest Profit

Among the analyzed sub-categories, **Copiers generate the highest profit**.

This makes Copiers an important contributor to profitability and suggests that the company could further focus on this sub-category to strengthen its profit performance.

### Customer Segment Analysis

Customer segments were compared based on sales and profit to understand which groups contribute most to overall business performance.

This can help the company make better decisions regarding customer targeting and resource allocation.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Continue focusing on the **Technology category** because of its strong sales and profitability.
- Consider additional investment and resources in the **West region**.
- Investigate the factors responsible for the strong increase in sales over time.
- Explore opportunities to increase the contribution of the **Copiers sub-category**.
- Use customer-segment performance to improve targeting and business strategies.
- Monitor both **sales and profit** when evaluating business performance rather than focusing on sales alone.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from **data cleaning and preparation to exploratory analysis, visualization, and business insights**.

Excel was used for data preparation, Python Pandas was prioritized for exploratory analysis and answering business questions, and Power BI was used to present the findings through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis identified **Technology as the strongest overall category, the West region as the leading sales region, and Copiers as the most profitable sub-category**, while also examining sales trends, customer segments, discounts, and loss-making products.
